# Project instructions

- Use descriptive branch names without the `codex/` prefix. This is the owner's explicit preference.
- Keep MaliPet independent of the owner's other projects and keep the first release modest.
- The soft 2D character pose sheet at `art/concepts/malipet-pose-sheet-v1.png` is approved. Use Walk 2 as the preferred walking likeness and the black/neutral-charcoal coat, not brown or sepia versions.
- Preserve both approved sleeping poses and the exact placement and subtle scale of the two white chest hairs. The separately saved source images are the reference for these details.
- Include occasional brief tongue-out animation variations with irregular timing; do not repeat the expression on every loop.
- Keep original personal reference photographs out of the public repository.
